How to Get to PCU

Find Petra Christian University in Surabaya with a map, transport tips, and nearby city and mall locations.

Petra Christian University

Petra Christian University (PCU) is located at Jl. Siwalankerto 121-131, Surabaya 60236, Indonesia. The campus sits in a central area of western Surabaya with easy access to major roads and local transport.

City Center

Approximately 20 minutes from Surabaya city center and the business district.

Airport

Around 30 minutes from Juanda International Airport by taxi or ride share.

Shopping & Malls

Close to Tunjungan Plaza, Pakuwon Mall, and BG Junction, all within 30-40 minutes.

Transport

Accessible by Grab, Blue Bird taxi, and local bus services across Surabaya.

Nearby Landmarks

  • PCU is within easy reach of Surabaya's Western Corridor.
  • Tunjungan Plaza and Pakuwon Mall are the closest major shopping centers.
  • Surabaya's major train stations are 20-30 minutes away by car.
  • Peak travel time around the campus is typically 25-35 minutes during rush hour.

Travel Tips

For first-time visitors, use ride-hailing apps or a trusted taxi from Juanda Airport. Ask your driver to use the campus address and verify with the university's official address.

If you plan to explore the city, Surabaya offers easy access to restaurants, malls, and cultural areas from the area around PCU.

Preparation & Arrival Guide

Everything you need before departure and during arrival in Surabaya.

Download Guidebook

Pre-Departure Checklist

  • Confirm your student visa and travel documents.
  • Arrange airport transfer and arrival logistics.
  • Pack essentials, local SIM card, and currency for initial expenses.

Arrival Guide

  • Arrive at Juanda International Airport and use a trusted ride service to PCU.
  • Check in with the International Office upon arrival for orientation support.
  • Confirm your accommodation arrangements before leaving the airport.

Health Insurance

We recommend students have either Health & Travel Insurance from their home country before arriving in Indonesia. This is the preferred coverage for international students during the exchange period.

Make sure your plan includes medical evacuation, emergency care, and COVID-19 coverage if available.

First Week Checklist

  • Register at the International Office and attend orientation events.
  • Confirm your class schedule and campus facilities tour.
  • Open a local bank account if needed and get a local SIM card.

International Community Outreach Program

Transform communities while transforming yourself through meaningful service learning in rural Indonesia.

Program Overview

The International Community Outreach Program (COP) is an integrated service-learning program that combines academic study with meaningful community engagement. Students partner with local communities across rural Java to address real social challenges while developing cross-cultural competence, leadership skills, and global citizenship.

Duration

2-8 weeks intensive immersion

Location

Rural villages across East Java

Community Focus

Education, health, environment, livelihoods

Four Pillars of COP

Academic Learning

Interdisciplinary classroom study exploring development issues, cultural studies, and social entrepreneurship before field placement.

Service & Social Action

Direct engagement with community partners on high-impact projects addressing local needs—education, health, environmental sustainability.

Cultural Immersion

Live alongside Indonesian host families, learn the language, and deepen understanding of local values, traditions, and worldviews.

Reflection & Growth

Regular guided reflection on personal transformation, ethical leadership, and commitment to global social responsibility.

Focus Areas of Community Engagement

Education & Youth Development

English language support, STEM mentoring, scholarship assistance, and skills training for underserved youth.

Environmental Sustainability

Community-led conservation initiatives, waste management education, sustainable agriculture practices, and climate adaptation projects.

Health & Wellness

Health awareness campaigns, maternal and child health initiatives, nutrition education, and community health worker training.

Economic Empowerment & Livelihoods

Microenterprise development, vocational training, women's economic cooperatives, and sustainable income generation initiatives.

Social Justice & Rights

Advocacy for vulnerable populations, gender equality initiatives, and community empowerment for marginalized groups.

Typical Program Flow

1

Pre-Departure Preparation (1-2 weeks)

Orientation on cultural sensitivity, Indonesian language basics, program objectives, and expectations. Team building and partner briefing.

2

Community Integration (Days 1-3)

Arrival in host village, meet host family, community welcomes and orientation. Begin casual language practice and cultural learning.

3

Active Service & Learning (Days 4-End)

Daily project work with community partners. Regular classroom sessions with facilitated reflection. Evening cultural exchanges and language practice.

4

Closing & Reflection

Community celebration honoring partnership and friendships. Final reflection on personal transformation and commitments to continued impact.

What You'll Gain

Cross-cultural competence — Deep understanding of Indonesian culture, values, and development context
Leadership & resilience — Navigate challenges, lead teams, adapt to new environments
Practical skills — Project management, community engagement, language proficiency
Global citizenship — Commitment to social justice and sustainable development
Meaningful relationships — Lasting friendships with Indonesian peers and global community partners
Real impact — Contribute to sustainable development in partner communities

Who Can Participate?

Requirements

  • • Currently enrolled student or recent graduate
  • • Minimum age: 18 years old
  • • Good physical and mental health
  • • Commitment to service-learning principles
  • • Flexibility and openness to new experiences

Ideal Participants

  • • Passionate about social impact and development
  • • Interested in cross-cultural learning
  • • Problem solvers with adaptability
  • • Team players with cultural sensitivity
  • • Motivated by making a difference

What is a Joint Degree?

A Joint Degree is a single degree awarded by two universities (PCU and an international partner) to a student who has completed an integrated program of study. The degree recognizes the student's successful completion of requirements at both institutions and is officially conferred by both universities.

Key Features

  • • One jointly awarded degree
  • • Integrated curriculum
  • • Study split between institutions
  • • Recognized by both universities
  • • Single graduation ceremony

Benefits

  • • Streamlined program duration
  • • Dual academic perspectives
  • • International recognition
  • • Cost-effective pathway
  • • Enhanced global credentials

What is a Double Degree?

A Double Degree program allows students to earn two separate but related degrees from two institutions. Students complete full degree requirements at both PCU and an international partner university, receiving two distinct diplomas recognized by each institution.

Key Features

  • • Two separate degrees awarded
  • • Independent curriculums
  • • Extended study period
  • • Study time at both institutions
  • • Two graduation ceremonies

Benefits

  • • Two internationally recognized credentials
  • • Deep expertise in two institutions
  • • Enhanced career opportunities
  • • Global professional network
  • • Maximum academic flexibility

Visa & Immigration

Essential information about visa requirements and immigration procedures for international students studying at Petra Christian University in Indonesia.

Student Visa Types

Student Visa (KITAS)

Limited Stay Permit for students enrolled in Indonesian educational institutions. Valid for up to 1 year, renewable.

Social-Cultural Visa

For participants in exchange programs, research, or cultural activities. Maximum stay of 90 days.

Business Visa

For internship programs or research activities with business purposes.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • University acceptance letter from PCU
  • Health insurance certificate
  • Financial proof (bank statements, sponsorship letter)
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Medical certificate

Application Process

1

Apply to PCU

Receive official acceptance letter from Petra Christian University.

2

Prepare Documents

Gather all required documents and have them legalized if necessary.

3

Apply for Visa

Submit visa application at Indonesian embassy or consulate in your country.

4

Immigration on Arrival

Complete immigration procedures at the airport and register with local immigration office.

Important Information

Processing Time: Visa applications typically take 3-7 business days at Indonesian embassies.

Visa Fee: Varies by nationality and visa type. Contact your nearest Indonesian embassy for current fees.

Health Insurance: Mandatory for all international students. Must cover at least $10,000 for medical expenses.

Reporting: Must report to local immigration office within 10 days of arrival for KITAS extension.

Need Help with Your Visa?

The International Office at Petra Christian University provides guidance and support for visa applications and immigration procedures. Contact us for assistance.
